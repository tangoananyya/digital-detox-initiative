About the chapter

A chapter built on competitive excellence

Founded in 2013, Turner Fenton DECA has grown into one of the largest and most accomplished chapters within Ontario DECA. Each year, more than 400 students apply to join, with roughly 100 members representing our school across business management, finance, marketing, entrepreneurship, and hospitality events.

Since 2017, our chapter has earned over 1,500 awards at the regional and provincial level, and qualified more than 100 students for ICDC.
